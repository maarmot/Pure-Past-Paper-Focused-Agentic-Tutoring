Use any row that answers your question. A page, chapter section, or timestamp makes it easier to revisit the same argument.

Keep a prediction made beforehand distinct from an interpretation formed afterward. In a project that allows learning records, [the argument-session starter](argument-session.template.json) can carry the learner's actual claims, reasons and remaining question into the next session.
