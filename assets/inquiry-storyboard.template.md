# Inquiry storyboard template

This is a blank design aid, not a script the learner must follow. Leave unknown learner facts unknown. Keep solution material separate from any question-first view.

## Question and obstacle

- Learner's actual question, or clearly labeled proposed opening:
- What is difficult to see, distinguish, connect, or infer:
- Concrete phenomenon the learner can inspect:
- Existing attempt or prediction, if supplied:
- Desired explanation, investigation, or decision:
- Course scope only if relevant:

## World, observation, and controls

- What remains physically fixed:
- What the learner can change:
- What is measured, with units and timing:
- Assumptions and model limits:
- What is merely a display transformation:
- Initial, reveal, reset, and reduced-motion states:

## Possible branches

- Learner's own follow-up questions, quoted or faithfully paraphrased:
- Current question they want to pursue:
- Proposed tutor questions, labeled separately from anything the learner actually asked:

| Learner action or question | What changes and what stays fixed | Visible consequence to investigate | Optional explanation or mathematical certificate |
|---|---|---|---|
| To be proposed | To be specified | To be checked | Separate from initial view |

- Original question and state to bookmark if the inquiry branches:
- A way to compare or return:
- A useful unresolved question, unless the learner supplies their own:

## Checks established before evaluation

| Claim or control behavior | Expected result and independent basis | Counterexample or failure case | Observed result and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| To be specified | To be established | To be chosen if useful | Unrun |

Check mathematical correspondence, labels, axis scales, meaningful extreme values, and actual reveal/reset behavior. Keep proof, numerical illustration, and schematic motion distinct. Record media and interaction checks in the production evidence.

## Possible stopping point

- A supported explanation, discriminating observation, counterexample, or precise open question:
- Learner's actual response and assistance, if observed:
- Optional next exploration or exam-practice connection:
- Unrun checks and unresolved uncertainty:

Do not require a transfer task or formal session close while the learner wants to keep exploring.
