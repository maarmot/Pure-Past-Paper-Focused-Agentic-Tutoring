\section*{Disagreement and uncertainty}
Compare definitions, populations, assumptions, units, and methods before combining results. Seek evidence that could reject the preferred explanation.
\field{Contradictory evidence and unresolved question}
\section*{Next discriminating test}
Specify the expected result before running the test, the method, and the result that would change the conclusion.
\field{Test, expected result, and rejection criterion}
\section*{References and limits}
Use precise titles, authors or institutions, dates, and links. Mark unavailable raw data or incomplete inspection. Do not convert a missing check into a positive finding.
