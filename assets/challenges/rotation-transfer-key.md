# A sensor with one wire · tutor key

At t = n/20 seconds the phases in turns are 6n/20 and 14n/20 = n − 6n/20. They produce positions reflected across the horizontal axis. All horizontal readings therefore match. A sequence of any length leaves both motors possible under the stated measurement model.

Use the first nonzero sample, t = 1/20 s. The vertical coordinate is positive for 6 and negative for 14 turns/s, since 6/20 of a turn lies above the horizontal axis and 14/20 below. At time zero both vertical readings are zero, so having a second wire alone does not ensure a useful observation.

**Suggested scoring, 5 points.** Give 2 for the phase or geometric reflection relation for all sample times, 1 for the conclusion about any horizontal sample count, 1 for a usable time and 1 for distinct predicted vertical readings. A numerical trace over a few frames can support a conjecture; completeness requires a general argument such as periodicity with a complete period. The changed decision is which coordinate to measure while the shutter times stay fixed.
