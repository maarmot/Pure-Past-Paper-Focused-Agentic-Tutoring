# A different contract for the rail

Original generated transfer problem. Work on a fresh page with pencil and paper. The client now pays for the longest possible response time to a single call anywhere on a 4 m rail. Leftward travel is at 2 m/s, rightward travel at 1 m/s, and the cart starts instantly. A dock can be built anywhere in the two closed intervals [0, 2] m and [3.5, 4] m, measured from the left end.

Find the best dock location and its guaranteed worst response time. The installer proposes finding the best location on an unrestricted rail and moving to the nearest legal point. Decide whether that proposal works here and justify the decision.
