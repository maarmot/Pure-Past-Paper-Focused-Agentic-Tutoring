# One extra photograph

Generated exam-style practice in signals and measurement. Suggested time 12 minutes with pencil and paper. Course alignment and completion time are unvalidated.

A single marked point rotates counterclockwise at a constant rate of at most 55 turns per second. It starts at the right-hand edge of a circle. An ideal camera records the full position instantly every 1/20 s, starting at time zero. From each photograph to the next, the observed position advances by 1/4 turn counterclockwise around the circle.

1. Find every rotation rate consistent with the photographs. Explain why the list is complete.
2. The camera can take one extra photograph. Measured from time zero, choose from 1/20 s, 1/40 s and 1/60 s. Which choices, if any, let you distinguish **every** rate on your list? Defend your decision.
3. Your colleague removes the speed limit from the specification but keeps the possible shutter times. Would the measurement you chose still identify a unique rotation rate? Give evidence for your answer.
