# One extra photograph · tutor key

At each regular exposure the point moves through a quarter turn plus some whole number of turns. In turns per second this gives (1/4 + k)/(1/20) = 5 + 20k. The direction and 55-turn/s bound admit k = 0, 1, 2, so the candidates are 5, 25 and 45 turns/s. Checking just three values without deriving the integer family leaves completeness open.

The predicted positions below are fractions of a counterclockwise turn from the start, with whole turns removed.

| Extra time (s) | 5 turns/s | 25 turns/s | 45 turns/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1/20 | 1/4 | 1/4 | 1/4 |

Only 1/60 s distinguishes every bounded candidate. Without the speed limit, any compatible f and f + 60 turns/s agree at every original exposure and at this extra time. The faster point adds three turns per regular interval and one at the extra measurement. For example, 5 and 65 turns/s remain indistinguishable.

**Suggested scoring, 10 points.** Give 2 for the phase constraint and integer family, 2 for applying direction and bound to give the complete set, 3 for correctly resolving the three shutter options, 1 for explaining that all candidates need distinct positions, and 2 for an unbounded counterexample that matches both sets of measurements. An answer with all three rates and all three phase rows but no derivation of the family, no uniqueness explanation and no unbounded example earns 5/10 (2 + 3). Accept drawings or clock-face reasoning that establish the same facts.
