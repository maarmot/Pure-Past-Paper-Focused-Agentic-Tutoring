# Across fields

Use this for an essay subject, or when the learner wants a method from one subject to help in another. For a physics, circuits, thermodynamics, or programming problem, [tutoring](tutoring.md) is enough; open this only if you are unsure what the independent check or the transfer problem should be.

The loop does not change: attempt, first unsupported step, repair, retry, transfer. Five things change with the field.

| Field | An attempt is | The first unsupported step looks like | Independent check | Transfer problem | Marked by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physics, mechanics | A diagram, a chosen system, then equations | A force, sign, or axis the diagram does not justify | Units; a limiting case; a conservation law the solution did not use | Same principle on a different surface or system | Answer and method marks |
| Circuits, signals | A labelled circuit or block diagram, then equations | A reference direction, or an assumption of linearity or steady state | Power balance; the DC and high-frequency limits; a second method | Same network idea in another topology or domain | Answer and method marks |
| Thermodynamics | A boundary, the states, the process path | The wrong kind of system, or a property used outside its region | Mass and energy close; entropy generation is not negative | Closed to open system, or a different fluid model | Answer and method marks |
| Programming, algorithms | Code or pseudocode | The first line where a trace on a small input goes wrong | Edge cases; comparison with a brute-force version on small inputs | Same technique under a changed constraint | Tests, or a key on written papers |
| Proof and "show that" questions | The chain of steps | A step that does not follow from the ones before | Each step's validity; the result is given, so reaching it proves nothing | Same technique, different statement | Method marks |
| Essay subjects | A plan with thesis and argument, or timed paragraphs | A premise asserted without support, an objection raised and not weighed, a conclusion that does not follow | The strongest objection; the marker's criteria | Same argument form, different question or source | Level descriptors or a rubric, by best fit |

## Essay subjects

A plan is a legitimate attempt. Do not ask for a full essay before helping.

Read the whole response before judging it. For a request about the weakest point, connect a sound move to the unsupported conclusion or objection that most needs work. For a full marking request, cover each consequential criterion. Follow the actual marking scheme, including analytic point allocations when the scheme uses them.

An essay may admit several defensible positions. One retry is to state the strongest objection to the thesis and answer it. Offer that after the requested feedback.

For practice, give a requested model paragraph or essay immediately. For assessed work, follow the actual course rules and the learner’s stated purpose.

Give a band or mark only with the marker's criteria in front of you: the exam board's level descriptors or the instructor's rubric, supplied by the learner or found in a source you opened. Quote the phrase the placement rests on and call it a tutor's estimate. Without criteria, say so and give the diagnosis with no band. Criteria and weightings differ between subjects, boards, and institutions; take them from the current specification, never from memory.

An unaided transfer in an essay subject is a new question, answered without help, in which the weakness named last time does not recur.

Use the brief phrase needed to identify a claim, together with its location. Paraphrase the surrounding argument. On a first submission, record the observable target and what the response demonstrates. On a retry, the target can be the named repair. Use qualitative feedback when the available work cannot support a bank outcome.

## What carries over

Worth prompting across engineering subjects, because the move is the same and only the surface differs: unit and dimension checks, limiting cases, order-of-magnitude estimates, choosing a system boundary (free body, control volume, node or loop), conservation framing, and listing assumptions with where each one fails.

Between engineering and essay subjects, these carry only when someone makes them explicit: stating premises, finding the first unsupported step, separating a claim from its evidence and from the link between them, and testing a claim against a counterexample the way one tests a formula at a limit.

These do not carry: content knowledge, notation, formulas, a field's stock problem types, essay conventions, and method-mark habits applied to a best-fit essay.

In a cross-subject reply, identify one reasoning move the learner can reuse and one thing the new discipline requires them to supply. For a history essay, testing a causal inference transfers; the dates, provenance, and representativeness of the historical evidence need their own support. Do not tell a learner that practising mechanics will improve their essays, or the reverse. Say what was observed.

## Bridge

At most once in a session, after a repair, you may ask: "Where have you made that move before?" Use the question bank only if it is already open. Never ask it before giving the help.

Record a bridge only in the learner's words. A bridge the tutor stated is not evidence, and a named bridge does not fill the transfer gate; only an unaided solution to a changed problem does.
