# Research without overstating evidence

Use this workflow when research or system revision is the requested task. Ordinary tutoring does not need a new creator study.

## Audit before expanding

Inventory the current skill, course files, scripts, dependency lock, source notes, rendered artifacts, and prior verification. Record what you actually ran separately from retained reports. Preserve useful work and version meaningful revisions.

Probe tools needed for the current stage: metadata/transcripts, actual browser playback and screenshots, local Manim Community, text typesetting, FFmpeg, PDF rendering, and optional image generation. An executable on PATH is availability, not a successful use. Resolve local paths before treating a tool as absent. Do not install globally as a workaround.

When playback fails, try a permitted browser or official written/source counterpart. Captions can still support language analysis. They cannot establish on-screen layout or motion. Record access failures and proceed with work that does not depend on them.

## Sample by teaching job

For a newly requested full study, aim for 6-8 videos and 3-5 meaningful moments per video if access and budget permit. The target is not a completion claim. Choose creators for the teaching contrast they offer; a name appearing in a plan does not mean it was inspected. The earlier study's candidates and coverage are listed in [provenance](provenance.md).

Choose moments that introduce a problem, link an equation to a picture, explain a limit or transformation, defeat a misconception, or move to formal reasoning. Record why each sample was selected. Add a contrasting sequence that might reject an emerging rule, such as a useful formula-first explanation.

## Capture an evidence unit

1. Verify creator, title, original URL, and source version. Do not merge differing examples from a written adaptation and a video.
2. Seek in the actual video and wait for the intended frame to load. Capture and open the screenshot for pixel inspection.
3. Record capture date/time with zone, visible player timestamp, target time, file path, and direct timestamped URL. Mark approximate seeking or a player overlay.
4. For motion, inspect playback or ordered distinct frames. Record the observed sequence; a still cannot establish easing, speed, or duration.
5. Pair nearby captions where available, preserving their source and cue times separately from frame times.
6. Write separate observations, transcript claims, interpretations, limits, and an original adaptation idea. A generated illustration, thumbnail, or written diagram is not a captured frame.

Use [the visual evidence template](../assets/visual-evidence.template.json). A record with no frames remains `incomplete`. The validator checks shape and local file existence, not whether an image is genuine or whether someone looked at it. Create labeled contact sheets only from genuine captured frames, with source IDs and timestamps; retain originals. Never copy a creator's voice, narration, or whole scene into an original lesson.

## Convert observations into decisions

Turn each observation into a row: the principle, the evidence it rests on, the limit of that evidence, and the original use you propose. Look for one counterexample before adopting a rule. The rows from the earlier study, and what that study did not cover, are in [provenance](provenance.md); describing them here is not the same as having inspected them in a new workspace.

## Report bounded conclusions

Separate what ran, what was observed, what was inferred, and what was implemented. Preserve failures and disagreements. A small purposive creator sample supports reusable ideas, not causal claims about retention or an optimal production formula. A tool benchmark supports only its measured workload, environment, and comparison.
