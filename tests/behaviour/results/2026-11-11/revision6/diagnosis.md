All fail the old criterion requiring a strength before criticism. That ordering rule conflicts with this request and with the skill's explicit weakness-first instruction. The new evaluation does not impose it; the historical results remain unchanged.

The additional candidate partial belongs to reply B, revision 5 sample 3: "That fits the instructor's Level 3 description ... on the plan as written." The matching descriptor is quoted correctly. ## Beam-model advice

[E08](../../2026-11-10/final/cases/E08.json) asks whether a steel cantilever 200 mm deep and 600 mm long always permits Euler-Bernoulli theory. Reply C, revision 5 sample 2, correctly computes an illustrative shear/bending ratio, then closes: "The actual section, loading, quantity of interest and allowed error determine whether that matters for your estimate." Criterion e is PARTIAL because the next action is implicit. Naming determinants does not tell the learner what to do next.

The old criterion b calls slenderness "the deciding condition." It is a screen. Quantity, support/load configuration, shear rigidity and the allowable error determine adequacy. The six [T2 follow-ups](../../2026-11-10/final/cases/T2.json) already revise the recommendation for a first bending estimate when span/depth changes to 30. That success must survive; it is not a universal accuracy guarantee.

Revision 6 routes approximation questions explicitly, compares omitted contributions when the inputs suffice, defines the error denominator, and selects the next decisive missing input. The [independent reference](beam_reference.json) integrates bending and shear virtual work. The new cases include a changed depth that flips the verdict, a different support/load coefficient, and an unknown depth whose interval straddles the tolerance threshold. These are stipulated elementary models, not validations of actual hardware.

## Limits of the diagnosis

These are observed reply omissions, rubric conflicts and plausible instruction gaps. The historical records cannot identify the causal mechanism behind a particular model sample. Five new development samples per version all passed the frozen development criteria. The held-out comparison therefore needs to be allowed to report equal or mixed performance. Adding instructions is not by itself a measured improvement.
